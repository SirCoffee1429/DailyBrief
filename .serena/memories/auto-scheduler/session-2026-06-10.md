## Final architecture decisions (owner-confirmed)
- **Solver-first, AI-second** (Ryan reversed my first AI-led draft): deterministic solver
  builds the draft honoring hard constraints; Gemini reviews after — candidates for
  unfilled slots + plain-English reasons; AI fills re-validated before landing.
- Availability = **free-form, no rule types**: per-day rows (status/time-window/note);
  `week_start NULL` = recurring default week, dated Monday = override, **no row = fully
  available**, Monday=0. Crew self-serve from kitchen side. No varies_weekly flag needed.
- 40h cap + per-run "Allow overtime" toggle; soft target hours; `draft|published` status
  on schedules (Phase 3); labor cost out of scope.
